Tips for long recordings
  • Use Chrome or Edge on a laptop or desktop. That's where this page is tested most, and they tolerate more memory use than Safari.
  • Up to about an hour works well on most computers. For longer interviews, split the recording into one-hour parts (QuickTime Player's Edit → Split, or Audacity), transcribe each part, and join the downloads.
  • If the page reloads or stops partway, tick “One step at a time” under Advanced settings and try again. It's slower but uses much less memory.
  • Keep the computer awake and plugged in, and leave this tab open until the downloads unlock. Closing or reloading the tab loses the transcript, so download it as soon as it's finished.
